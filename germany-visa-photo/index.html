Germany visa photo: white, light grey or medium grey background

German missions do not all word their photo rules the same way. The photo-sample sheet published by the German Missions in the United States (germany.info) asks for a plain, bright background, ideally neutral grey: light grey suits dark hair and medium grey suits light hair. It also gives a face height of 32–36 mm, 70–80% of the photo.

The German Embassy in London’s visa FAQ instead asks for a white or off-white background, photos no older than three months, printed at 35 × 45 mm. Check the instructions of the mission that handles your application.

When you choose Germany, the app:

  • offers three backgrounds — white, light grey (preselected) and medium grey — so you can follow your mission’s instructions;
  • targets a face height of 32–36 mm of the 45 mm photo height and exports a 420 × 540 px JPEG of up to 512 KB;
  • shows a Germany note on which background suits which hair colour;
  • links to the German photo-sample sheet from its in-app guide list.

Price

Processing and previews are free, and you can retry as often as you like before buying. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks one JPEG export ($1.99 on the U.S. App Store; prices vary by region). No subscription.

The app’s interface is available in English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, Español, 日本語, Deutsch and Français, and the photo is processed on your iPhone.

iPhone · iOS 17 or later

Follow the photo editing instructions from the consulate or visa centre handling your application.
